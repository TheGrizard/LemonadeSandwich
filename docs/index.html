LEMONADE SANDWICHGENERATIVE FIRMWARE FOR THE M-VAVE FM-1

Press REC and the FM-1 rolls a whole new piece: sounds, key, tempo, generators and modulation across all four tracks, each roll with its own character. Everything Tessera does is still here underneath.

Version 1.0 · lemonade-sandwich-1.0.fwsc (one-core fallback: lemonade-sandwich-1.0-1core.fwsc)

The rolls

Pick one in MENU > REC BUTTON (hold HOME for the menu: it's the top row; KNOB 2 picks the roll, hold HOME again to leave), then press REC. Press it again for another. The rolls are in key and dry, ready for your own effects.

FALL

Warm ambient on TRIO's triangles: a drone, chords and two falling melodies, slow.

TIDE

A slow sub groove across the bar, falling leaves, an ORACLE melody, a triangle bed and a quiet noise.

WOZZA

Minimal piano and synth, slow, off the grid, tape-worn.

ORBIT

Bouncing, orbiting patterns, fast and musical.

LOOPS

Four sparse loops of different lengths drifting apart: felt piano, two warm pads and a glass.

HALO

Warm and hazy: a slow line, a soft voice on its triad, a canon below, a held halo.

MIRROR

HALO's mirror: glassy and digital, microtonal now and then.

PHASE

One bright cell on two tracks, one moving a step ahead every few bars.

CRATE

An old funk or soul record to sample, no drums: keys, bass, a hook, strings or a choir.

COSMIC

Synthwave: a composed minor phrase, driving bass, wide pads, a lead, an arp.

STATIC

A wall of atonal noise in four held layers with a brooding Reese bass.

FORGE

Dark polyrhythmic techno: a deep kick, never four on the floor, Euclidean bass, hats.

CHAOS

FORGE noisier and more synthetic: crushed FM kicks, metal and noise.

FRACTURED

Glitchy, broken beats under FALL's warm pad.

RND

A whole new piece, random and strange.

MENU > DRIFT (OFF, SLOW, FAST) lets a roll change while it plays: new riffs, melodies, parts dropping out and coming back. OFF keeps it exactly as rolled. Every part stays editable afterwards.

The engines

TRIO: three oscillators and a filter, for basses, pads, leads and the warm triangles. ORACLE: two oscillators, poly-mod and a 4-pole ladder filter. MACRO: Mutable Instruments Braids' textures, voices, FM and drum models. Effects: TAPE, SPIT, ECHO, REVERB, FILTER and DRIVE.

Install

  1. Open the installer in Chrome or Edge on a computer (or an Android phone), or in the Web MIDI Browser app on an iPhone or iPad.
  2. Connect the FM-1 by USB and follow the page. Keep it connected until it finishes.

It installs over M-VAVE's official firmware, Tessera, or another FM-1 firmware, on FM-1s with either flash chip, and can put M-VAVE's firmware back. Use it at your own risk; save a Complete backup from the editor first if you have projects to keep. From a terminal: python3 tools/fm1_install.py lemonade-sandwich-1.0.fwsc.
